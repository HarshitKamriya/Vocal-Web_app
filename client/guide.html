User Guide

Welcome to VocalPath.

After opening the app, listen carefully to the instructions.

To start navigation, tap the bottom left button on your screen.

Your camera will open. Slowly move your phone in all directions so the system can scan your surroundings.

Once scanning is complete, speak your destination clearly.

After the target is locked, follow the voice instructions to move left, right, or forward.

When you hear navigation completed, you have reached your destination.

Double tap anywhere on the screen to return to the home screen.
